## Data Analytics Portfolio

Welcome to my data analytics portfolio! I am a final-year BSc Computer Science student majoring in Mathematical Statistics at the University of Free State, with an interest in data analytics, statistical modelling, machine learning, and business intelligence.

This portfolio showcases projects where I use data to identify patterns, evaluate performance, build predictive models, and communicate insights.

## Projects

### 1. Credit Risk Analysis and Default Prediction

**Tools:** Python, SQL Server, Machine Learning, Power BI

An end-to-end credit risk project exploring loan default patterns, data preparation, statistical modelling, machine learning evaluation, and decision thresholds.

**Key areas:**
- Data understanding, cleaning, and feature engineering.
- Statistical analysis and credit risk modelling.
- Comparison and evaluation of machine learning models.
- Model interpretation and decision-threshold analysis.
- SQL-based data analysis and dashboard reporting.

[Explore the Credit Risk Analysis project](./credit-risk-analysis/)
### 2. Retail Sales Performance and Profitability

**Tools:** Power BI, Data Analysis, Data Visualisation

An interactive business analytics project exploring retail sales, profitability, product performance, discount patterns, and customer purchasing trends.

**Key areas:**
- Sales and profit performance analysis.
- Product and category profitability.
- Discount patterns and their relationship with sales.
- Business insights presented through dashboard screenshots and a project report.
